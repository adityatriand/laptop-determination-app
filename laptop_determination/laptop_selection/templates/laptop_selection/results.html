What is AHP (Analytical Hierarchy Process)?
🎯
Multi-Criteria Decision Making

AHP is a structured technique for organizing and analyzing complex decisions. It breaks down a decision into a hierarchy of criteria and alternatives, then uses pairwise comparisons to determine priorities.

⚖️
Pairwise Comparison

Each criterion (Price, RAM, SSD, HDD, CPU) is compared against every other criterion to determine their relative importance. This creates a comparison matrix that calculates priority weights.

📊
Consistency Check

The system calculates Consistency Ratio (CR) to ensure your preferences are logical. CR ≤ 0.1 means your choices are consistent and reliable.

🏆
Final Ranking

Each laptop gets a score by multiplying its performance in each criterion by the criterion's weight. Higher scores indicate better overall matches to your preferences.
